Designed for Personal Wellness & Sovereignty

Everything you need to track vital health indicators without third-party surveillance.

🛡️

Local-First Data Ownership

Your records live solely in your local SQLite database on your device. Zero telemetry, zero analytics beacons, zero ads.

❤️

Blood Pressure & Vitals

Track systolic, diastolic, pulse, and mean arterial pressure with American Heart Association classification markers.

🥗

Nutrition & Food Journal

Log daily meals using the integrated USDA FoodData Central database. Track calories, macros, and personal targets.

⚖️

Weight & Metabolic Estimates

Log weight trends, BMI, and calculate estimated Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).

🔄

Private Sync Options

Synchronize seamlessly between your PC and phone via local folders, direct Wi-Fi LAN peer sync, or private Google Drive sandbox.

🎨

Adaptive Theming

Modern Material 3 user interface with dynamic theme switching, high-contrast chart visualizations, and responsive layouts.

Cross-Platform User Experience

Consistent, responsive Compose Multiplatform experience across desktop workstations and mobile devices.

Blood Pressure Trends

Visual AHA classification ranges and historical trend analysis.

Nutritional Meal Journal

Searchable USDA food items, macro breakdowns, and calorie tracking.

Compound Health Visualizations

Multi-metric comparative charts for weight, calories, and blood pressure.
